# Design notes

**Idea:** the warp-knit structure. Each role on Home has a vertical chain of knit stitches
(a warp thread). Picking a skill turns the matching threads hi-vis yellow on indigo.
Sections are separated by a horizontal row of stitches.

**Tokens:** greige `#F3F4F1`, indigo-black `#16202C`, indigo `#2A3A8F`, surface `#E6E8E3`,
muted `#5A6470`, hi-vis `#E3F200` (highlights only). Archivo (headings) + Public Sans (body).
Square corners; light mode, because readers are business owners during the working day.
Playfulness 1–2. Motion: only the fade when filtering; off with reduced motion.

**Sources for content:** `project/materials/Maulik_Haria_Resume copy.pdf`, `linkedin Profile.pdf`,
`project/industry_interview.md`. Phone number and GPA left out on purpose.
