Why textiles

[PLACEHOLDER] Draft written by Claude from my interview answers. Rewrite it in my own words.

My family's company, Best Knitting Mills, makes warp-knit polyester fabric and processes fabric for B2B customers in home, apparel, protective textiles and athleisure.

I learned the business by sitting in on meetings about product development, strategic investments, expansion plans and operations. What held my attention most were the big decisions: what goes into them, the research they need, and making sure the right processes are in place afterwards.

My internships in investment banking, equity research and consulting taught me to size markets, value businesses and present to boards. At Kusumgar I worked on strategy in technical textiles and learned to look for under-penetrated markets where most products are imported. I want to find gaps like that for our own business.

After I graduate in May 2027, I'm joining the business full time. My goal is to expand it and improve operations across all of our manufacturing plants, and to build partnerships with businesses around the world.
